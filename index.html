Summary

Certified Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (D365FO) functional consultant with an MSc in Finance & International Business and 14+ years of combined finance and ERP experience. Since 2018 I have delivered 6 end-to-end D365FO implementations, including 2 covering 10+ countries, and worked on 10+ projects in specific phases of implementations. My focus is the business processes around property management, AR, AP and product information management, plus master data, configuration and data migration, from requirement workshops through to tested go-lives.

Before working with D365FO projects, I spent five years at Situs analysing and underwriting more than €5 billion of loan portfolios for investors such as Deutsche Bank and Cerberus. Structured, detail-oriented and comfortable taking ownership, with a strong interest in AI and automation in finance.

  • Presented the analysis and recommendations to the bank's Credit Committee.
  • Analysed and checked commercial real estate valuations in the Baltic States and CIS countries: large commercial properties valued at €1–50 million.

Associate / Analyst, Situs

Denmark · Dec 2012 – Jan 2018. Strategic business solutions for the finance and commercial real estate industries. Clients include Deutsche Bank, Cerberus, BoAML, AXA, AIG, Bank of China and Hyundai.

  • Underwrote and performed due diligence on seven secured performing and non-performing loan portfolios with an aggregate value above €5 billion, including financial and investment analysis of corporate and commercial real estate transactions.
  • Translated analysis into investor advisory: clear conclusions and recommendations for senior decision makers.
  • Designed and built Excel models to underwrite and evaluate loan portfolios and underlying collateral.
  • Helped set up a loan-servicing platform for a €200 million portfolio in Latvia on behalf of Deutsche Bank, covering business plans, decision-making structure, IT system and SOPs.
  • Reviewed and optimised internal business processes, delivering significant time savings and fewer errors.
  • Worked with stakeholders from Analysts to VPs and Managing Directors across the Nordics, Germany, UK, US, France, China, Korea and Turkey.
  • Moved from Analyst to Associate in 2016.

Senior Functional Consultant, YAVICA
